# Sleeve — app icon

Motif: a record sleeve on the left, with a CD sticking halfway out of its
right side. The four abstract lines on the sleeve are the metadata the app
writes — the only hint at what it does.

Requirements: [`../Docs/ICON-BRIEF.md`](../Docs/ICON-BRIEF.md).

---

## Files

| File | Purpose | Geometry |
|---|---|---|
| `sleeve-icon-master.svg` | The original, four named groups | with squircle |
| `sleeve-icon-dark.svg` | Dark mode version, see below | with squircle |
| `Sleeve.icns` | Classic fallback, 16–512 px each @1x and @2x | with squircle |
| `layers/*.svg` | The four layers individually, import source for Icon Composer | borderless |
| `Sleeve.icon/` | Icon Composer document, design state | borderless |
| `preview-sizes.png` | Contact sheet for review, light and dark | with squircle |
| `preview-zoom.png` | Check view: small sizes enlarged pixel-exact | with squircle |

What "with squircle" and "borderless" mean is explained under
[Two geometries](#two-geometries).

The app does **not** use the version in this folder but
`Sleeve/Resources/Sleeve.icon` — the same file, written to both places by the
script so that Xcode's synchronized folder group finds it.

Do not edit any of it by hand. Everything is generated by:

```bash
./Scripts/build-icon.sh
```

The script calls `Scripts/make-icon-svg.py` (geometry and colors) and
`Scripts/make-icon-preview.py` (contact sheet) and needs `rsvg-convert`
(`brew install librsvg`) as well as `iconutil` from Xcode.

## Two geometries

The two target formats do **not** take the same picture:

**Classic (`.icns`, master, previews)** — the picture brings its own shape: a
squircle at `x=100, y=100, w=824, h=824, rx=184`, everything clipped to it,
100 px of room all around. That is what the brief asks for, and what macOS
expects of an `.icns`.

**Icon Composer (`layers/`, `Sleeve.icon/`)** — the layers have to fill the
whole canvas. macOS applies mask, depth, shadow and highlight itself. For
that the content is scaled up around the center by `1024/824 = 1.2427`, the
squircle clip is dropped, and the own drop shadow is left out — otherwise it
would lie twice under the system's.

Whoever uses the inset version as an Icon Composer layer gets an **icon
within the icon**: macOS puts its own shape around it, the own squircle sits
inset inside, and there is a dark frame in between. That is exactly where the
first attempt failed.

## Dark mode version

As required, only the lightest gradient stop is desaturated, the lower stops
stay, and the lines get a little stronger.

| | light | dark |
|---|---|---|
| Lightest gradient stop | `#8FE3FF` | `#97DFF7` (saturation −15 %) |
| Line 1 | 0.92 | 1.00 |
| Line 2 | 0.62 | 0.68 |
| Line 3 | 0.40 | 0.44 |
| Line 4 | 0.26 | 0.29 |

The line opacity rises by 10 % relatively, capped at 1.0 for line 1. The
difference is deliberately restrained — more would leave the palette.

## Optical correction

Arithmetically, 50 px of room remain on the left between squircle and sleeve,
69 px on the right between CD and squircle. The round CD edge tolerates less
margin than the straight sleeve edge, so the group of sleeve, CD and lines
moves by the permitted **10 px to the right** — afterwards 60 px left, 59 px
right. That is the only intervention in the given geometry.

## Review

Checked on the contact sheet and additionally by measurement.

| | Criterion | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | At 32 px sleeve and CD are two separate shapes | met, without contrast adjustment |
| 2 | At 32 px at least two lines visible as structure | met — **three** separate bands measurable, no simplified small variant needed |
| 3 | At 16 px the silhouette stays "rectangle with circle on the right" | met |
| 4 | Crystal facets not perceptible from 128 px down | **fixed**, see below |
| 5 | Drop shadow does not run over the squircle edge | met, alpha outside is exactly 0 |

## Pitfall: colors in `icon.json`

Icon Composer expects colors with a color space and a colon:

```
extended-srgb:0.2078,0.7059,0.9098,1.0000
```

A bare hex value like `#35B4E8` crashes `actool` with
`-[__NSPlaceholderArray initWithObjects:count:]: attempt to insert nil object`
— without any hint as to which field is meant. The conversion is done by
`srgb()` in `Scripts/make-icon-svg.py`.

---

### What had to be fixed

The facets were at first two sharp-edged polygons with opacity 0.055 and
0.038. Measurement: the deviation from the facet-less rendering was 11 of 255
channel steps at 1024 px **and** unchanged at 128 px. Large shapes lose no
amplitude when scaled down — they would have stayed as visible at 128 px as at
1024 px.

The facets now lie behind a blur (`stdDeviation="26"`) at opacity 0.05 and
0.032. That leaves a broad brightening of 9 of 255 steps, but **no edge**: the
largest brightness jump between two neighbouring pixels from 128 px down is
only 1 step above that of the plain background gradient. Noticeable at
1024 px, no longer recognizable as structure at 128 px.
